About AFFILUXA

Empowering careers through quality education and guaranteed placements

Who We Are

AFFILUXA is a premier training institute located in Muzaffarpur, Bihar, dedicated to providing world-class education in optical fibre technology. Founded and led by Mehedi Raza Naqvi, we are committed to bridging the gap between education and employment.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to equip young individuals with the skills and knowledge they need to succeed in the rapidly growing telecommunications industry, while ensuring they have guaranteed job placements upon completion of their training.
